ABOUT ME

Building Products with Engineering, Architecture and Purpose

I'm a Fullstack Developer with 4+ years of experience building web and mobile applications and APIs. I work from understanding business needs and defining the technical solution through implementation, testing and production delivery, with a focus on scalable architecture, security and code quality.

My strongest backend ecosystem today is NodeJS, especially with TypeScript and NestJS; on the client side, I work extensively with React, React Native, NextJS and Expo. I have experience with layered architecture, Clean Architecture, SOLID, database integration and GraphQL, REST APIs, CI/CD, test automation, Redis caching and RabbitMQ messaging. I also work with C#/.NET and Python/FastAPI on backend features, and I have experience with mobile applications featuring offline-first, OTA updates and feature flags.

My work includes software architecture and technical leadership: I lead the design of solutions from requirements gathering to the technical documentation for front-end, back-end and mobile, for discussion and validation with the team before development. I define API contracts, entities, interfaces, business rules and forms, screen responsibilities, and state management and persistence strategies, aligning technical decisions with product and business needs together with the team.

Throughout my career, I've worked on ERP systems, financial products and mobile applications, including products published to the App Store and Google Play.
